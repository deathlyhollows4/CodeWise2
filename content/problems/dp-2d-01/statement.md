A rectangular grid uses 0 for an open cell and 1 for a closed cell. Start at the top-left cell and finish at the bottom-right cell. A move goes exactly one cell right or one cell down. You may only stand on open cells. Return the number of distinct move sequences modulo 1000000007. The starting and finishing cells are part of the route: a closed endpoint makes the answer zero. An open 1 by 1 grid has one empty move sequence. A grid with no rows or no columns has no route and returns 0. Rows and columns keep their input order. Do not enumerate all routes for large grids.
