Vertices 0 through n - 1 form a directed capacitated network. Source is 0 and sink is n - 1. Each row [u,v,capacity,unitCost] is one original edge. Choose an integer flow for every edge between 0 and its capacity. At every intermediate vertex, total inflow equals total outflow. Net outflow at the source and net inflow at the sink must both equal required. Return the smallest sum of flow*unitCost over original edges, or -1 if exactly required units cannot be sent. Original unit costs are nonnegative, so profitable circulation cycles do not exist. With required=0, return 0. Parallel edges are allowed; self-loops are not. Reverse residual edges are algorithmic cancellation choices with negative cost, not extra original paid edges. Return a 64-bit integer in Java/C++. Do not merely send one unit or maximize flow regardless of required.
