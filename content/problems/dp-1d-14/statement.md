Return the largest product of any nonempty contiguous segment of values. The segment may contain a single value and may not skip any interior position. Values can be negative, zero, or positive. An empty segment with product 1 is not allowed. Multiplication by a negative value can turn an earlier minimum into a new maximum, while zero can separate useful segments. Use a 64-bit integer for every intermediate product. The limited input length and magnitude ensure that every contiguous product is exact in both signed 64-bit integers and safe JSON numbers.
