A rectangular binary grid uses 1 for land and 0 for water. An island is a maximal group of land cells joined by up, down, left, or right moves. Count how many different island shapes occur. Two islands have the same shape exactly when translating every cell of one by the same row and column offsets makes its cells equal to the other. Do not rotate or reflect shapes when comparing them. Multiple copies of one shape count once. Diagonal touching does not connect land. Empty grids or grids with no land return 0. Do not change the input grid.
