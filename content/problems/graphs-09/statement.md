A directed graph has vertices 0 through n-1. At a vertex with outgoing edges, a walk may choose any outgoing edge. A terminal vertex has no outgoing edge and stops a walk. Return, in increasing vertex order, exactly those starting vertices for which every possible walk eventually stops. A vertex is unsafe if some outgoing choices can reach a directed cycle, even when another choice reaches a terminal. Cycles and their predecessors are unsafe. Self-loops are cycles. Disconnected components are evaluated separately. The empty graph returns []. No duplicate directed edges occur.
