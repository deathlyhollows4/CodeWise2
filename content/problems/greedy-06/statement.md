Travel forward from position 0 to target, using one fuel unit per distance unit. Start with startFuel and an unlimited-capacity tank. Each stations entry is [position, fuelAvailable]; positions are strictly increasing. At a reached station, you may take its entire fuel amount once, or skip it. Taking fuel counts as one stop. Stations beyond target are allowed but unnecessary. Return the fewest refueling stops that make target reachable, or -1 if no route can reach it. Exactly reaching a station or target with zero fuel remaining is allowed; a station at position 0 is reachable even with no starting fuel. If starting fuel already reaches target, return 0. You cannot take fuel from a station before you can physically reach its position.
