Complete one task on every day, in order, using workstation A or B. costsA[i] and costsB[i] are the costs of completing day i at those workstations. If two consecutive days use different workstations, add switchCost once for that change. You may choose either workstation for the first day without a switching charge, and finish at either workstation. Return the smallest total cost of all daily tasks and all changes. Empty arrays return 0. Switching does not skip a day or change the order of tasks. All costs are nonnegative. The local input arrays may be modified; grading checks only the returned total. Use a signed 64-bit total.
