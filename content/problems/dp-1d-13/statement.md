Return the largest sum of any nonempty contiguous segment of values. A contiguous segment uses every position between its first and last index. You may choose a single value. You may not skip interior positions or choose an empty segment. In particular, an all-negative input returns its largest individual value, rather than 0. The input is nonempty. Return a 64-bit integer and use a wide accumulator. Aim for O(n) time and constant extra state.
