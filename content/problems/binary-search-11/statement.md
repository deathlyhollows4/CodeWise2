Weights must be shipped in their original order. Each item is indivisible. A day ships a contiguous next group of items with total weight at most capacity; once a new day starts, no later item may move into an earlier day. Return the smallest capacity that ships all items within days days. You may use fewer days and leave later days unused. For an empty list, return 0. There is no per-job service rate or rounding of time: only the whole weights in each daily group count. Use a 64-bit result because the total weight can be large. Local decoded input arrays may be modified; only the returned result is graded.
