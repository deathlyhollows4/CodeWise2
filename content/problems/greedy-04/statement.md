Each jobs entry is [deadline, reward] for one distinct job that takes exactly one time slot. Slots are numbered 1,2,...; a job with deadline d must finish in some slot numbered at most d. Only one job can occupy a slot. You may omit any jobs, and jobs are otherwise available from the start. Return the maximum reward earned. Duplicate entries represent separate jobs, so multiplicity is preserved; the order of entries has no meaning. Rewards are nonnegative. There are no specified start times or varying durations: unlike interval selection, you choose the slot of each selected unit-time job. Empty input returns 0. Use a 64-bit total when many large rewards fit.
