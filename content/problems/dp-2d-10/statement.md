Two collectors traverse a rectangular grid of nonnegative rewards. Both begin on row 0: one at column 0, the other at the last column. At every move each collector goes to the next row and changes column by -1, 0, or +1, staying inside the grid. They stop after visiting the last row. Return the greatest total reward they can collect together. Each visited cell contributes its reward once, even if both collectors occupy it in the same row. Include their starting cells. They may meet, cross, or separate again. A grid with no rows or no columns returns 0. In a one-column grid, both collectors occupy the same cell on every row. Use a 64-bit integer accumulator in Java/C++.
