Two players take turns moving a token along directed edges. moves[u][v]=1 permits a move from u to v; 0 forbids it. targets[u]=1 marks a terminal target. Moving to a target wins immediately for the mover. Thus, when classifying an already-target position for the player whose turn it is, the result is LOSE: the game has already ended against that player. A non-target position with no outgoing move is also LOSE for its player to move. Outgoing rows of marked targets are ignored. Otherwise players alternate one legal move per turn. Infinite play is DRAW. Both players prefer their own WIN to DRAW to their own LOSE. Return one string per vertex in vertex order: WIN if the player to move can force a win, LOSE if the opponent can force that player to lose, and DRAW otherwise under optimal play. A self-loop or cycle alone does not imply a win or loss. Empty moves/targets arrays return[].
