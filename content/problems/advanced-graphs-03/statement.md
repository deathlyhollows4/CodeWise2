An undirected graph has vertices 0 through n - 1. Each row [u,v,risk] in edges is one connection with nonnegative risk. A route starts at vertex 0 and finishes at vertex n - 1. Its score is the greatest edge risk used anywhere on that route. Return the smallest possible score, or -1 if no route exists. With n=1 the empty route has score 0. With n=0 there is no route and return -1. You may revisit vertices, but nonnegative maximum risk cannot improve by adding a loop. Parallel offers and self-loops are allowed. A zero risk is a free-risk connection, not an absent edge. You do not need to return a route or connect unrelated vertices.
