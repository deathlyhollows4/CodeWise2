Project i requires your current capital to be at least capital[i]. Completing it adds profits[i] to your capital; the required capital is a threshold and is not spent. Each project can be completed once. Begin with initial capital, choose at most k projects in any feasible order, and return the largest possible final capital. Profits are nonnegative, and you may stop early when no project is affordable. The arrays capital and profits describe the same project positions.
