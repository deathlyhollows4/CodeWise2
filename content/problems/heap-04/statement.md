Workers are arranged in their original array order, and worker i costs costs[i]. Hire exactly k workers, one at a time. At each step, only the first candidates remaining workers and the last candidates remaining workers are eligible. If those windows overlap, a worker is eligible once. Choose the eligible worker with the smallest cost, breaking ties by the smaller original index. Remove that worker and repeat with the newly formed windows.

Return the total cost paid by this specified process. Do not choose the globally cheapest k original workers when some are not yet eligible, and do not optimize future tie choices: the original-index tie rule is mandatory. candidates may exceed the remaining worker count, making every remaining worker eligible. k = 0 returns zero, including for empty input. Use a wide total.
