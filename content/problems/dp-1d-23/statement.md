Three equal-length arrays describe jobs: job i starts at starts[i], lasts durations[i] time units and earns rewards[i] if completed. Starts are strictly increasing, so each job has a distinct start time; durations are positive. A job occupies the half-open interval [start, start+duration), so one job may end exactly when another begins. Choose any subset of nonoverlapping jobs and return the maximum total reward. Completing no jobs is allowed. Jobs cannot be split or partially rewarded. Reward is not a priority rule: taking the largest single reward or the earliest finish greedily can miss the best compatible combination. Use a 64-bit total; all reported answers remain JavaScript-safe.
