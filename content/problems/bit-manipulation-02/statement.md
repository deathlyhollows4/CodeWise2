Bits of word are numbered 0 through 31, with bit 0 the least significant. Return the unsigned integer represented by bits low through high, including both endpoints. The selected low bit becomes bit 0 of the result.

word is supplied as a nonnegative integer, even when bit 31 is set. Use a wide integer result: selecting the full field may return 4294967295. Do not interpret the selected bits as a signed two's-complement value.
