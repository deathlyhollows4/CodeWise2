A rectangular binary grid uses 1 for land and 0 for water. You may change at most one 0 cell into 1. Return the largest area of any island possible afterward. An island joins land by up, down, left, or right moves; its area is its number of cells. You may also make no change. A single flipped cell can touch the same existing island from several directions, but that island is counted only once. An all-land grid returns its full area; a nonempty all-water grid returns 1. Empty grids or grids with no columns return 0.
