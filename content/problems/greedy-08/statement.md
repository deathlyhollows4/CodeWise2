weights lists nonnegative weights of separate components. Repeatedly choose two current components, replace them by a new component whose weight is their sum, and pay that sum as the merge cost. Continue until at most one component remains. Return the minimum total cost over all pair-merge plans. Empty input and one component cost 0. Zero-weight components are allowed and still count as components. Equal weights represent distinct components; multiplicity matters, while the input order does not. This minimizes accumulated cost across all merges, not the final combined weight or the cost of only the last merge. Use 64-bit values for intermediate weights and total cost.
