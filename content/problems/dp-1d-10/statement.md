Give every indexed nonnegative value either a plus sign or a minus sign. Count sign assignments whose resulting total equals target, modulo 1000000007. Every item must be used exactly once with one sign. Equal values at different indices still have separate sign choices. Plus zero and minus zero are different choices, even though both contribute 0. The order of input items does not change the number of assignments. For an empty input, the empty assignment counts once for target 0 and not at all for any other target.
