Two signed integer lists are sorted in nondecreasing order. Together they contain at least one value. Find their combined median without merging all values. To represent half-integer medians exactly, return twice the median as an integer. For an odd combined length, double the middle value. For an even length, return the sum of the two middle values. Either list may be empty. Duplicates are allowed. Aim for O(log(min(left.length,right.length)+1)) time and constant extra space. Local decoded input arrays may be modified; only the returned result is graded.
