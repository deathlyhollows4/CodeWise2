prices[i] is the nonnegative price of one share on day i. Start with no shares and may hold at most one share. Each day you may buy, sell a previously held share, or do nothing; at most one action is allowed that day. After a sale on day i, you may not buy on day i+1. The earliest next purchase is day i+2. There is no transaction fee and no limit on the number of transactions. End with no shares and return the maximum total realized profit; doing no transactions is allowed. Empty or single-day prices returns 0. Selling and buying on the same day is not allowed. This cooldown is a temporal eligibility constraint, not a deduction from each transaction price.
