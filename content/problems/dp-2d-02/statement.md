Each cell of a rectangular grid has a nonnegative visit cost. Start at the top-left cell, finish at the bottom-right cell, and move exactly one cell right or down at a time. The total cost is the sum of every cell you stand on, including both endpoints. Return the smallest possible total. There are no closed cells, and you cannot move diagonally or leave the grid. A grid with no rows or no columns returns 0. Use a 64-bit integer accumulator in Java/C++; the answer can exceed a 32-bit integer. Input row and column order is meaningful.
