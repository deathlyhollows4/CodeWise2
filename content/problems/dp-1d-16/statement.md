Choose zero or more nonoverlapping substrings of text, each equal to a dictionary word. A dictionary word may be reused. Return the smallest number of characters left outside the chosen substrings. Uncovered characters may appear anywhere, including between two chosen words. Words must match consecutive original characters; they cannot jump over an uncovered character. Matching is literal and case-sensitive. Count unmatched characters, not dictionary words. The empty text returns 0. With an empty dictionary, every character is unmatched. Unlike complete dictionary segmentation, a text with no complete cover can still have useful partial coverage.
